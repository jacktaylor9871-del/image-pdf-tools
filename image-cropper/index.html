Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for passport and ID photo cropping in the US, UK, Canada, and Australia.

What size is a US passport photo?
2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm), 600 × 600 pixels at 300 DPI for digital uploads. Use the 1:1 preset above, center your head and shoulders, and download as JPG.
What size is a UK passport photo?
35 × 45 mm for print, or at least 600 × 750 pixels for online applications. Use the 35 × 45 preset above.
Is this cropper free? Do you upload my photo?
Yes, it is completely free with no sign-up and no watermark. Your photo never leaves your device — all cropping happens in your browser.
Can I crop to an exact pixel size?
Yes. Type the exact X, Y, Width, and Height in pixels in the side panel, set an output width if needed, and download in JPG, PNG, or WebP.
