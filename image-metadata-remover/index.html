Remove the hidden data from your photos before you share them

Every photo from your phone quietly carries your GPS location, your phone model, and the exact date and time it was taken. Add a photo below to see what yours reveals — then strip it all out in one tap.

Your photos never leave this device. Everything is read and cleaned right here in your browser — nothing is uploaded to any server.

JPG, PNG or WebP — add as many as you like. HEIC (iPhone) photos can be checked for metadata but not cleaned in most browsers.

What gets removed

  • GPS location — the exact spot where the photo was taken
  • Phone details — make, model and software version
  • Date & time — when the photo was captured
  • Camera settings — lens, exposure, flash and more
  • Editing history — XMP and app data from editors
  • Comments & hidden text — embedded notes and thumbnails

How it works

  1. Add a photo. The tool reads its hidden EXIF data and shows you everything it found.
  2. Tap “Remove metadata”. The picture is redrawn pixel by pixel, which leaves all hidden data behind — the photo itself looks identical.
  3. Download the clean copy and share that one instead. The cleaned file is checked again to confirm nothing is left.
Cleaned photos keep their original quality and orientation. Your originals are never changed — you always download a separate clean copy.
