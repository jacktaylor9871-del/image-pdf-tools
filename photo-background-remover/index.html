Tips for a good photo

  • Stand in front of a plain background — a single-color wall works best; it makes it easier for the AI to cut cleanly.
  • Even light on your face — take the photo facing a window, with no bright light behind you.
  • Face straight and clearly visible — glasses or hats can cut into the edges; check your form's rules.
  • Zoom in and check the result — look at the hair and shoulder edges, and fix any mistakes with the touch-up brush.
  • Dark clothes + white background — the contrast makes the photo look sharp and clear on the form.
