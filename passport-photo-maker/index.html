Passport photo rules — avoid a rejection

Use a plain, light backgroundStand in front of a plain white or off-white wall. There should be no people, furniture, patterns, or shadows behind you. This is required in the US, UK, Canada, and Australia.

Face the camera directlyBoth eyes open, mouth closed with a neutral expression, head straight — not tilted or turned. Your face should be centered and fill most of the frame.

No glasses, hats, or filtersSunglasses, hats, headphones, and beauty filters will cause a rejection. In the US and UK, everyday glasses must also be removed for passport photos.

No old photos or angled selfiesYour photo must be taken within the last 6 months. Avoid angled selfies — ask someone to take a straight, eye-level photo of you instead.

Frequently asked questions

What size passport photo do I need?

It depends on your country. United States: 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm). United Kingdom & Australia: 35 × 45 mm. Canada: 50 × 70 mm. Always check the size listed on your application first, then choose the matching preset above.

What size is a US passport and visa photo?

A US passport and visa photo is 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm). Choose the “US Passport & Visa” preset — your download will be 600 × 600 px at 300 DPI. For the online DS-160 visa form, also use the “Under 240 KB” download option.

What about UK, Canada, and Australia?

UK: 35 × 45 mm for passports and most visas — the same size is used for EU / Schengen visas. Canada: 50 × 70 mm for passports. Australia: 35 × 45 mm for passports. All three presets are included above at 300 DPI.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. Your photo is cropped and resized entirely inside your browser. There is no upload, no account, and no storage — everything is cleared when you close the page.

How many copies fit on a print sheet?

It depends on the photo and sheet size you choose — the exact count is shown next to the sheet selector. For example, a 2 × 2 inch photo fits 2 copies on a 4 × 6 inch sheet and many more on Letter or A4. When printing, set your printer to “Actual size / 100%” — not “Fit to page.”

How do I get a white background?

This tool crops and resizes your photo — it does not replace the background. For a compliant white background, take your photo in front of a plain white wall in bright, even light. That is the safest method for US, UK, Canadian, and Australian applications.
