Common reasons to convert

PNG → JPG: A portal, marketplace, or email rejects your PNG or says the file is too large. Converting to JPG at 85–92% quality usually cuts the size dramatically with no visible difference. Common for Indeed, LinkedIn, school portals, and seller uploads.

JPG → PNG: A website or application specifically requires PNG, you need a transparent-ready lossless copy before editing, or you need crisp text and logos that JPG would blur. Common for logos, signatures, and design work.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert a JPG to PNG for free?

Drop your JPG into the converter above. With “Auto” or “JPG → PNG” selected, it converts instantly in your browser — no account, no watermark, and no software to install. Click Download to save the PNG. It works the same on Windows, Mac, iPhone, and Android.

Does converting JPG to PNG improve quality?

No. A JPG has already lost some detail to compression, and converting it to PNG can't restore that detail. What you get is a lossless copy of the JPG exactly as it is now — useful when a website, school, employer, or government form specifically requires a PNG file.

Why convert PNG to JPG?

File size. JPG compresses photos very efficiently, so a photo saved as PNG often becomes 70–90% smaller as a JPG — small enough for upload limits on job sites, college portals, and marketplaces like Amazon and Etsy. Use the quality slider at 85–92% for the best balance of size and sharpness.

What happens to a transparent PNG background in JPG?

JPG can't store transparency, so transparent areas are filled with the background color you choose above — white by default, which is what most forms, documents, and ID uploads expect. If you need to keep transparency, convert to PNG instead.
