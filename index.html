Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions these tools get most often.

Are these image and PDF tools really free?

Yes. All 18 tools are free to use as often as you like. There is no sign-up, no trial, and no watermark added to your files.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Each tool works entirely in your web browser, on your own device. Your photos and PDFs never leave your phone or computer, which makes these tools a safe choice for ID documents, signatures and personal paperwork.

How do I make an image or PDF small enough for an online form?

For a photo, open the Image Compressor and pick a target size such as 100 KB or 200 KB. For a document, open the PDF Compressor and choose a compression level. Both show you the size before and after, so you can check it against the form’s limit before you upload.

Which passport photo size do I need?

The United States uses 2×2 inches (600×600 px). The UK and Australia use 35×45 mm, and Canada uses 50×70 mm. The Passport Photo Maker has all of these as one-tap presets at 300 DPI, plus print sheets for 4×6, Letter and A4 paper.

Can I combine a resume, cover letter and certificates into one PDF?

Yes. If they are already PDFs, use the PDF Merger and drag them into order. If they are photos or scans, use the Image to PDF Converter instead — it builds one PDF from your pictures.

Do the tools work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. They run in any modern browser — Safari, Chrome, Edge or Firefox — on phones, tablets and computers. There is nothing to install.
