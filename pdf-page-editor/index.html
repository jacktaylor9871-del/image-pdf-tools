Common questions

Does my PDF get uploaded anywhere?

No. The editing happens entirely in your browser on your own device. Your file never leaves your phone or computer, so it is safe for private documents too.

Can I undo a delete or a rotation?

Yes. Every deleted page has a Restore button, and rotating a page four times brings it back to where it started. You can also use “Reset all changes” to start over with the same file.

What if a page is upside down?

Tap the rotate button (↻) twice on that page — two 90° turns make 180°, which flips an upside-down page the right way up.

Is there a page or file-size limit?

There is no fixed limit, but very large PDFs (hundreds of pages) can be slow on older phones because every page preview is drawn on your device.
