Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert a PDF to JPG for free?

Upload your PDF above, select JPG as the format, choose your resolution, and click Convert PDF to Image. Each page becomes a separate JPG you can download one by one or all together as a ZIP — completely free, with no sign-up.

PDF to PNG vs. PDF to JPG — which is better?

Choose PNG when you need the sharpest text, line art, or a document that must stay perfectly readable — it's best for forms, certificates, and print. Choose JPG when file size matters, for example when a portal limits uploads to 200 KB, 1 MB, or 5 MB — just lower the JPG quality slider until the file fits.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. This converter runs 100% in your browser using JavaScript. Your file is never sent to a server, never stored, and never shared. That makes it safe for private documents like tax returns, bank statements, pay stubs, contracts, and photo ID.

What resolution should I choose?

72 DPI is fine for quick previews and thumbnails. 144 DPI (recommended) is ideal for most online forms and screen use. Choose 216 or 288 DPI if you plan to print the image or need very small text to stay crisp.

Can I convert a multi-page PDF?

Yes. Every page is converted into its own image, in order — for example, report-page-01.png, report-page-02.png. Use the Download All as ZIP button to get them all at once.

My PDF is password-protected. What should I do?

This tool can't open password-protected PDFs. Open the file in a PDF reader (like Adobe Acrobat or Preview on Mac), enter the password, and save an unlocked copy — then convert that copy here. Many US bank and credit card statements let you save an unprotected version this way.

Does this work on iPhone, Android, and Chromebook?

Yes. It works in any modern browser — Chrome, Safari, Edge, and Firefox — on Windows, Mac, iPhone, Android, and Chromebook. No app install or software download is needed.
