Tips for applications and uploads

  • Watch the size limit. Many job portals and email services cap attachments at 2–10 MB. Choose the “Small” quality setting and the PDF will usually fit comfortably.
  • Match the page size to the country. Letter is standard for forms in the US and Canada; A4 is standard in the UK and Australia.
  • Keep scans straight. Every card has a rotate button, so a sideways page never ends up sideways in your application.
