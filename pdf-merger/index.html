Privacy — where do your files go?

Nowhere. Your files are never uploaded. The merge happens entirely in your browser (using the pdf-lib library). Close the page and everything is cleared — no copy is kept on any server.

That's why it's safer for personal or sensitive documents than random upload-based sites.

Frequently asked questions

How many PDF files can I merge? ＋

There's no fixed limit. 10–20 typical files merge easily on a phone. Very large files (50MB+) may slow your browser down.

How do I change the page order? ＋

Use the ↑ ↓ arrows on the right of each file. On desktop you can also drag a file by its handle. Pages in the merged PDF will follow exactly this order.

Can I merge a password-protected PDF? ＋

No. If a PDF is password-protected or encrypted, the tool will show an error. Remove the password first — or get an unprotected copy from wherever the file came from — then add it again.

Will the quality drop? ＋

No. This tool joins pages without compressing them — text, images and quality stay exactly as the originals. The final file size will be roughly the total of all your files.

Do I need an account? ＋

No sign-up, login or payment needed. Just add your files, merge and download.
