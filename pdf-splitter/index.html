Common questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser — your file never leaves your device, so it is safe for private documents and works even on a slow connection.

How do I make a separate PDF of just one page?

Choose Pick pages mode, select only that page, and click Split. Or in Page ranges, just type that page number, like 7.

Will the quality or text change after splitting?

No. Pages are copied directly from the original PDF — text, images, links, and quality stay exactly the same. Nothing is re-rendered.

Will a password-protected PDF work?

Encrypted or password-protected PDFs will not open in this tool. First remove the password in your PDF reader and save a copy, then split that copy.
