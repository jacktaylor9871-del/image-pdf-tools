Tips for a clean scan

  • Use good light: take the photo in daylight or white light, and keep flash glare off the paper.
  • Shoot straight down: hold the phone flat above the page — a tilted photo stays tilted in the PDF.
  • Use a dark surface: put white paper on a dark table so the page edges stand out clearly.
  • Black & White mode gives the cleanest result for faint pencil writing and old paper.
