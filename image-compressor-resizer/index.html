Frequently asked questions

How do I compress an image to 100 KB?

Add your photo, keep Target Size mode selected, choose the 100 KB preset (or type 100), then click Compress Images. The tool automatically adjusts quality — and dimensions if needed — to bring the file to 100 KB or less.

Will compressing reduce image quality?

A little, but at 70–85% quality the difference is barely visible for forms, websites and email. Use PNG if you need completely lossless quality, but expect a larger file.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. All compression happens locally in your browser using your device. Your photos never leave your computer or phone.

What is the best format — JPEG, WebP or PNG?

JPEG is best for photos and form uploads. WebP gives the smallest files for websites. PNG is best when you need transparency or lossless quality, such as logos and graphics.
