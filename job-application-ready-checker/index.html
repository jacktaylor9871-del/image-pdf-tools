FAQ

Common questions

Are my photo and resume uploaded anywhere?

No. The check runs entirely in your browser. Your files never leave your device — close the page and everything is cleared.

What size should my photo be?

It depends on the form. US visa / passport photos are usually 600×600 px and under 240 KB. UK passport photos are 35×45 mm (about 413×531 px at 300 DPI). Indian government job forms commonly ask for a photo around 200×230 px (20–50 KB) and a signature around 140×60 px (10–20 KB). Pick the matching preset above — and always confirm against your own form's instructions, because limits change.

My photo is the right size but too big in KB. What now?

Use the Image Compressor / Resizer on this site: set the same KB target and download the smaller file, then check it again here.

Can I check a resume that is not a PDF?

This checker reads PDF resumes only, because most job portals ask for PDF. Convert a Word or image resume with the Image to PDF Converter first, or save it as PDF and upload it here.
