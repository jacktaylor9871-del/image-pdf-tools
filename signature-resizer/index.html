How to get a signature that never gets rejected

  1. Sign on plain white paper — no lines, no notebook paper. Use a blue or black pen and sign clearly, the way you sign on official documents.
  2. Photograph it straight — hold your phone directly above the paper in good light. Avoid shadows and tilted angles.
  3. Upload it here and pick a preset for your form type, or enter the exact width, height and KB limit from the application instructions.
  4. Download and check — the green check above confirms your file is within the size limit before you submit your application.

Common questions

Why do online forms reject my signature image?

Almost always one of three reasons: the pixel dimensions don't match what the form requires, the file is larger than the KB limit, or the photo is blurry or gray. This tool fixes the first two exactly, and the “darker ink” option helps with the third.

What size should my signature image be?

Check the upload instructions on your form — they state the required size, for example “400 × 150 pixels, max 50 KB”. Common requirements are 300–400 px wide for job, bank and university applications. If no size is given, the Job Application preset (400 × 150 px, under 50 KB) works well for most online portals in the US, UK, Canada and Australia.

Is my signature safe here?

Yes. Resizing happens entirely on your device in your browser — your image is never uploaded to a server, and nothing is stored anywhere. No account or sign-up is needed.

Can I use this for contracts and PDF documents?

Yes. Resize your signature here, download the JPG with a clean white background, and insert it into Word, Google Docs or a PDF editor. For a transparent background, use a PNG signature maker instead — this tool is built for forms that require JPG on white.

My signature looks too small in the frame. What should I do?

Keep “Auto-trim” turned on and lower the white margin slider. If it still looks small, retake the photo closer to the paper so your signature fills more of the picture.
