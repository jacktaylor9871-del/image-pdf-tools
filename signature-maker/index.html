Typed signatures use a handwriting font — they look neat, but for official forms a hand-drawn signature is always the safer choice.

How to use it

  1. Draw or typeSign in the box above. On a phone, use your finger or a stylus — write at your natural size, not too small.
  2. Fix mistakesUse Undo for the last stroke, the Eraser for a small part, or Clear to start again. Pen blue looks most like a real pen on paper.
  3. Download the PNGThe file is cropped tight around your signature with a transparent background — ready to insert into a PDF, Word file or online form.

Where a transparent signature PNG helps

Job & visa forms

Many online application forms ask you to upload a signature image. A transparent PNG blends into the form instead of showing a white box.

PDFs & documents

Insert the PNG into a PDF or Word document exactly where the signature line is — no printing, signing and scanning needed.

Email & letters

Add it at the end of a letter or under your name in an email for a personal, handwritten touch.

Your signature is created entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server and no copy is kept. Keep the downloaded PNG somewhere safe, the same way you would guard a photo of your real signature.
